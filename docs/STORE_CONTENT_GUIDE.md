# VERDENA Store Content Guide

This folder documents the content structure used by the V3 storefront.

## Content areas

- `../data/products.json` — product catalog and merchandising metadata.
- `../data/collections.json` — primary shop-by-space collections.
- `../data/journal.json` — editorial article metadata.
- `../data/faq.json` — customer-support FAQ content.

## Production checklist

1. Replace demo contact details with the real business information.
2. Connect a production checkout/payment provider before taking payment.
3. Confirm image licenses and product availability.
4. Add shipping, returns, privacy, and terms pages for launch.
5. Replace the demo email-order workflow with a secure order backend or commerce platform.
