# VERDENA — Botanical Living

> **Bring the outside in.**

VERDENA is a premium botanical-living storefront built around a simple idea: indoor plants should feel like part of the architecture, not an afterthought.

The project combines living greenery, sculptural planters, botanical accessories, and quiet home objects into an editorial shopping experience designed for modern interiors.

---

## 🌿 Brand Direction

VERDENA is intentionally different from a traditional garden-center website.

Instead of organizing the experience around technical gardening supplies alone, the brand treats plants as **living interior design**.

The visual language is built around:

- Deep botanical greens
- Warm mineral neutrals
- Terracotta accents
- Natural stone and ceramic textures
- Editorial typography
- Calm negative space
- Large, carefully framed photography
- A refined, gallery-like product presentation

The goal is to make the website feel closer to a modern interiors publication than a conventional plant catalog.

---

## ✦ The VERDENA Experience

The storefront is organized around the way people actually bring plants into their homes.

### Living Plants
Indoor greenery selected for different spaces, light levels, and moods.

### Sculptural Planters
Ceramic, stone, terracotta, and textured vessels designed to complement the plant rather than compete with it.

### Botanical Care
Thoughtful tools and essentials for watering, pruning, feeding, and maintaining healthy plants.

### Plant Stands
Raised forms that give greenery more presence and help create visual rhythm throughout a room.

### Small Greenery
Compact plants for desks, shelves, bedside tables, and smaller spaces.

### Botanical Objects
Decorative pieces inspired by natural materials, organic forms, and slower living.

### Photography Principles

Photography is one of the most important parts of the VERDENA experience.

Every product image should follow these rules:

- One subject per image
- Product fully visible
- Centered composition
- Consistent proportions
- Clean, realistic photography
- No screenshots
- No website UI inside photographs
- No prices embedded in images
- No buttons or labels embedded in images
- No collages for individual product cards
- Consistent lighting and visual temperature

This separation keeps the **photograph clean** while allowing the website interface to control product names, descriptions, prices, and calls to action.

## 🌱 Product Philosophy

Every VERDENA product should earn its place through at least one of three qualities:

### Living
It introduces natural life into the home.

### Useful
It solves a real care, storage, planting, or display need.

### Beautiful
It contributes to the atmosphere of the space even when the plant is not the focus.

The strongest products should ideally satisfy two or all three.

## ⚡ Performance Standards

VERDENA should remain visually rich without becoming unnecessarily heavy.

Recommended standards:

- Compress all images before deployment
- Keep logos below 1 MB
- Prefer optimized JPEG/WebP photography
- Avoid unnecessary external libraries
- Use descriptive image filenames
- Provide meaningful `alt` text
- Lazy-load below-the-fold images
- Avoid duplicate image assets
- Keep individual product photographs independent

The target is a premium visual experience with fast loading.
